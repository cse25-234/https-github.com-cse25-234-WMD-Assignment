ABOUT DTEF

Our Mandate


The Department of Tertiary Education Financing (DTEF) is mandated to provide financial assistance to eligible students pursuing tertiary education ensuring equitable access to higher learning opportunities for all citizens.

Mission


To efficiently manage and administer tertiary education funding through innovative systems, strategic partnerships and transparent processes.

Vision


To be a premier provider of sustainable tertiary education financing solutions that drive national human resource development.
