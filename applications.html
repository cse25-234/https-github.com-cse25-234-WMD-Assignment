SPONSORSHIP APPLICATIONS

students should pick which application they are eligible for below

Local Placement

For students seeking sponsorship for undergraduate programs within local public and private institutions.

Top Achievers

For high-performing students pursuing elite international programmes and universities.Applicants should have atleast attained 6A*s at BGCSE/IGSCE, 3As at AS, 3As at A level.

Re-sponsorship

For students who previously held sponsorship and are seeking to restart or change programmes.The student have not currently benefited from re-sponsorship.
