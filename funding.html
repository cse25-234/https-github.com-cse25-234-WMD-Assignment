Eligibility requirements

how to apply for sponsorship

Citizenship


  • Must be a citizen of the country
  • Provide valid National Identity Card (Omang)
  • Dual Citizenship documentation (if applicable)

Age Requirements


  • Minimum age: 18 years
  • Maximum age for new applicants: 35 years
  • Special considerations for post graduate applicants

Academic Requirements


  • Minimum 36 points in BGCSE or equivalent
  • Credit in English Language
  • Admission Letter from a recognised institution
